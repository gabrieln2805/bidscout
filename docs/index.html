The rules this tool holds itself to

  1. Anything it checks, you must be able to check. Every claim on this page is reproduced by a command in the repository, not asserted in a document.
  2. "We could not tell" never becomes "you do not qualify". A missing figure, an unreadable sentence, a sum in another currency or a tender split into lots produces CHECK. Only a gate where both numbers are known and they do not meet can produce NO-GO.
  3. The raw data is kept for ever. A parser rewrite is replayed from stored payloads, never by polling the portal again.
  4. The repository is the project. Work that is not in it does not exist.
